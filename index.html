Our Services

Rice

We are a trusted exporter of premium-quality rice offering Non-Basmati Rice Basmati Rice Brown Rice and Broken Rice to global markets.

Our sourcing process ensures consistent quality hygienic handling and compliance with international food safety standards.

We supply Non-Basmati and Broken Rice primarily to Africa and China while Brown Rice and Basmati Rice are exported to Europe and Saudi Arabia.

With efficient logistics and strict quality control we ensure timely delivery and customer satisfaction across all destinations.

Agri-Products

We export a wide range of high-quality agricultural products including Groundnut Cumin Sesame Seeds and DOC Cattle Feed.

These products are directly supplied to Vietnam China Bangladesh Indonesia and Korea ensuring quality compliance and timely delivery.

In addition to exports we import commodities such as Mutter Chickpeas and Masoor Dal from Russia Canada Ukraine and African countries to meet domestic demand.

Salt

We export premium-grade salt products including Sea Salt / Raw Salt Crystal Salt Iodised Salt and Double and Triple Refined Salt.

Our salt exports meet international quality standards and are supplied to markets across Indonesia Africa Sri Lanka and Bangladesh.

Strict quality control and efficient logistics ensure purity consistency and on-time delivery for all salt products.

Scrap

We are actively engaged in the export of ferrous and non-ferrous metal scrap including HMS 1 & 2 Pig Iron Copper Scrap Aluminium Scrap Brass Scrap and Stainless Steel Scrap.

Our scrap materials are sourced and processed to meet international quality and recyclable standards.

We cater to major markets across Asia Europe and the Middle East supported by reliable logistics and compliance-driven operations.
